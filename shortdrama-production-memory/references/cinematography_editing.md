# Cinematography, Editing, Montage

## Shot Continuity

- Maintain three-dimensional spatial continuity.
- Keep screen direction, eyeline, body direction, prop hand, and motion axis consistent.
- Avoid jump cuts unless violent action, earthquake, or explicit visual shock justifies them.
- For movement, start the next shot from the previous shot's ending body position or motion state.
- Use 动接动 for continuous action and 静接静 when motion pauses.

## New Scene Rule

Every new scene starts with a wide or establishing shot to clarify:

- geography
- character positions
- props
- entry/exit direction
- screen axis

Then move to medium, close, or detail shots as needed.

## Camera Description Checklist

Include, as applicable:

- 景别: 中/近/特写/大特写 mainly; 全景/大全景/上帝视角 as support.
- 机位与高度: position, height, horizontal angle.
- 镜头参数: focal length, aperture/depth intent.
- 运动方式: push, pull, pan, track, crane, handheld, steadicam, rail, drone, orbit, whip.
- 运动路径与幅度: from where to where, distance, arc radius, angle.
- 速度与节奏: slow push, even speed, acceleration, hard stop, breathing motion.
- 对焦与主体切换: follow focus target, rack focus timing.
- 构图变化: thirds to center, blocked to open, frame-within-frame.
- 动作点与落点: which action starts the camera and what information point ends it.

## Montage Use

Use montage to connect visible facts so the viewer infers relation:

- person looking + object/space = viewer understands what is being watched
- close detail + reaction = emotional implication
- opposing subjects = conflict or parallel pressure
- environmental insert = transition, time, pressure, or hidden information

Do not use montage to add events outside the source text.

## Action and Impact

Actions must obey anatomy and ergonomics. For hits, spell impacts, sword contact, explosions, or shock:

- show cloth movement
- show body recoil or balance recovery
- show dust, sparks, energy fragments, or object vibration
- define the cut point, e.g. when blade touches shield, when foot lands, when light reaches face

## Foreground Rule

Only include foreground description when the shot size is 中景 or 近景. The foreground must come from the current scene and remain blurred.
